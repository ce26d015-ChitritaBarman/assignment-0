a) Remote and Local – Local is the copy of the project on computer where you actually edit files and Remote is the version on GitLab that other people can pull from.
b) Forking – Forking is making own independent copy of someone else’s repository.
c) Cloning – Cloning is downloading a repository to computer so you have a local working copy.
d) Push–Pull – Push uploads local commits to the remote repository; pull brings new commits from the remote to local.
e) Commit – A commit is like saving your project with a small note of what chages we made.
