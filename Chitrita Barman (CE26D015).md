 **a) Remote and Local:** Local is the copy of the project on your computer where you edit files. Remote is the version on GitLab that other people can pull from.

 **b)Forking:** Forking means making your own independent copy of someone else’s repository.

 **c)Cloning:** Cloning means downloading a repository to your computer so you have a local working copy.

 **d)Push–Pull:** Push uploads local commits to the remote repository. Pull brings new commits from the remote repository to your local computer.

 **e)Commit:** A commit is like saving your project with a small note describing the changes made.
